# 五任务训练指令

client只传task；以下是当前训练合同的英文，不在client临时改写。

| task | 本体 |
|---|---|
| book | Piper/3b |
| basket | Piper/3b |
| yifu | G2 |
| dengpao | G2 |
| diguan | G2 |

## book

Use the left arm to pick up the book from the table. Move the active camera arm to scan the bookshelf and find an empty slot, then align the book with that slot using the left arm and insert it.

## basket

Use the left arm to lift one corner of the towel covering the basket while moving the active camera arm to look inside the basket. Use the right arm to take the corn out of the basket and place it on the table, then use the left arm to cover the basket with the towel.

## yifu

Raise the right arm and take the clothes off the hanger. Then raise the left arm and use the right hand to place the clothes over the left arm.

## dengpao

Raise the left arm and hold the lamp stand steady. Move the right hand to the light bulb, unscrew it, and place it in the box on the right.

## diguan

Use the left hand to pick up the test tube from the rack and the right hand to pick up the dropper from the bottle. Move the dropper above the test tube opening, then squeeze it to drop liquid into the test tube.
